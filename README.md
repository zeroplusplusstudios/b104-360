# B-104 · 360° views

360-degree views of the B-104 flat (4 BHK): stand at a point, look all round, and walk to the next point by the
rings on the floor. A map shows where you stand. It also works with a phone in a VR box.

Illustrative 3D visualisation; furniture and finishes are indicative.

## How to open

Open https://zeroplusplusstudios.github.io/b104-360/ in Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox.

- Phone: tap **Look around** and turn the phone (or drag), tap a ring to walk. For a VR box, tap **VR box**,
  turn the phone sideways and put it in the box; look at a ring to walk, look down for the map.
- Laptop: click **Look around**, drag to look, click a ring to walk.
